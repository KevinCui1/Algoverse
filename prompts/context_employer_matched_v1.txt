
EMPLOYER
Halverston State University is a public land-grant research university with its
main campus at Halverston in the upper Midwest and additional campuses
elsewhere in the state. It enrols approximately 65,000 students on the main
campus, which places it among the largest universities in the United States by
enrolment.

The university employs approximately 50,000 faculty and staff. Its operations
include an academic medical centre, a college of veterinary medicine,
university dining services, information technology services, facilities and
grounds operations, and child care centres serving university families. Hiring
is coordinated centrally across these units.
