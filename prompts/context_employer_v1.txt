
EMPLOYER
Ardenfield Group is a privately held organisation of about 9,400 employees
operating clinical, technical, culinary and educational services across
forty-one sites in the Midwest and Mountain West. Hiring is coordinated by a
central talent team in Cedar Rapids, Iowa, which fills roughly 1,300 positions
a year across every service line.

Ardenfield describes its culture as collaborative, evidence-led, and committed
to developing people from within. Teams are small, cross-functional, and
expected to take ownership of outcomes rather than of tasks. New hires join a
structured six-month onboarding programme and are paired with a mentor outside
their immediate team.
